**Twenty minutes. That's all your legs have been asking for.**

You know the feeling. The last hour of the shift when your calves are made of
concrete. The day after a long run. The flight that left your ankles puffy and

Recovery Compression Boots are the same kind of sequential air compression
system pro teams and physical therapy clinics have used for years — now built
for your living room. Slide them on, press start, and let the air do the work.

**How it feels**

A slow wave of pressure builds at your feet, rolls up your calves, past your
knees, and releases at your thighs. Then it starts again. It's rhythmic,
warm and deeply relaxing — most people say the first session is the best
twenty minutes they've had all week.

**Why people keep using them**

- **Zero effort.** Sit down, zip up, press one button. No stretching, no
  foam roller, no wrestling with a massage gun you can't reach.
- **Full-leg coverage.** Four overlapping chambers work your feet, calves,
  knees and thighs — not just one spot.
- **Your pressure, your pace.** Adjustable intensity and multiple modes, so
  it's as gentle or as firm as you want it.
- **Built to disappear into your evening.** Quiet enough to use while you
  watch TV, read, or take a call.
- **It pays for itself.** A single recovery session at a clinic runs about
  $75. This is yours, unlimited, at home.

**Who it's for**

Runners and lifters who train harder than they recover. Nurses, servers,
stylists and tradespeople who spend ten hours on their feet. Frequent flyers.
Anyone over 50 whose legs just feel heavier than they used to.

**What's in the box**

Pair of full-leg compression boots · Control unit with digital display ·
Connecting hoses · Power adapter · Carry bag · Quick-start guide · 1-year
warranty
